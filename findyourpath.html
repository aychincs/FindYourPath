      About Find Your Path

      Why We Created Find Your Path

      Many students have dreams but don't know where to start. Some cannot afford expensive consultations. Others may receive incomplete or inaccurate information.

      We believe every student deserves access to reliable information and the opportunity to discover their own future.

      Our Mission

      To make career and university guidance more accessible by providing students with free, reliable, and organized resources.

      Our Vision

      A future where every student has the freedom and opportunity to choose their own path.
